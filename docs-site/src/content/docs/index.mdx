Swarm ID is sign-in and storage for apps built on [Swarm](https://www.ethswarm.org/). Your users
get one account that works in every Swarm app, unlocked with a passkey, a password or an Ethereum
wallet, in the browser they already have. Your app gets a signed-in user and somewhere to put their
data, with no servers, no session store and nothing to install.

Under the hood the account is a recovery phrase, so it can be restored on any device. Nobody has
to look at one to get started, though. Pick a password or a passkey and there is no wallet
anywhere in the flow.

Try it on [the demo app](https://swarm-demo.snaha.net). Press **Connect** in the sidebar and you
have an account two minutes from now. The demo then lets you upload a file, publish a feed and
share something with one chosen reader, which is roughly what any app on Swarm does.

## One account, every app, every device

Swarm ID is also where people manage that account. The identity site lists the apps they have
connected, lets them disconnect one, and holds their **drives**: prepaid storage on Swarm that any
connected app can write to.

A drive is bought once and follows the account. Sign in on a second laptop and the same drives are
there; upload from either and it lands in the same place. Storage belongs to the user and is paid
for by the user, so your app never holds a payment method or a storage budget for anyone. If you
would rather pick up the bill, a [subsidised gateway](./subsidised-gateway/) does that.

## What your app gets

- **Sign-in in one call.** The library opens the popup, waits for the user and hands you the
  connection.
- **Storage through the same connection.** Upload, download, publish a feed, encrypt for a chosen
  set of readers. Every Swarm request goes out on the user's behalf, from the user's drive.
- **Keys you never hold.** Each app receives its own key, derived for that app alone. Two apps on
  one account cannot read each other's data, and losing your app's key cannot expose the account.
  The secret that controls the account never leaves the Swarm ID site.
- **Every major browser.** Chrome, Firefox and Safari, desktop and mobile, no extension. This is the
  part that is usually hard on the web, and it is checked on real devices.

The identity site at [swarm-id.snaha.net](https://swarm-id.snaha.net) is hosted for you, so an
integration is a package install and a few lines. Everything is open source, and you can
[host your own](./self-hosting/) when you want the sign-in page on your domain.
