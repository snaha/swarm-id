My Top Recommendations

1. Storage Credit ⭐

Why: Clean, professional, widely understood. Works well in both technical and user-facing contexts. Easy to pluralize and discuss ("I need more storage credits").

2. Capacity Voucher ⭐

Why: Emphasizes both the prepaid nature AND the capacity aspect. Professional but accessible.

3. Storage Lease ⭐

Why: Best captures the time-bound nature. Clear that it's temporary and has terms. Works well technically.

4. Storage Pass

Why: Modern, streamlined, accessible. Works great in UI/UX. Less formal but very clear.

5. Proof of Capacity (PoC)

Why: Technical, blockchain-native terminology. Clear what it proves. Good for documentation.

Considerations

- "Storage" vs "Capacity": "Storage" is more user-friendly, "Capacity" is more technically precise
- Avoid "Batch": Too technical/database-y, doesn't convey value to users
- Time component: Consider names that hint at temporal nature (Lease, Term, Tenure)
- Blockchain context: Terms like "Proof", "Certificate", "Token" work well in Web3
